# Support

Thank you for using **SEAR**! If you need help, have questions, or want to report a problem, here are the best ways to get support:

## 📖 Documentation
Before asking a question, please check the [README.md](README.md), [ARCHITECTURE.md](ARCHITECTURE.md), and [FAQ.md](FAQ.md). Many common questions are answered there.

## 🐛 Bug Reports
If you encounter a bug, please open a [Bug Report Issue](https://github.com/mahdiklidari190/SEAR/issues/new?template=bug_report.md). Be sure to include:
- Your Python version and OS.
- The exact URL or sitemap you were analyzing.
- The full error traceback.

## 💡 Feature Requests
Have an idea for a new SEO check or report format? Open a [Feature Request Issue](https://github.com/mahdiklidari190/SEAR/issues/new?template=feature_request.md).

## 💬 Community Discussions
For general questions, "how-to" guides, or to share your experience, please use the [GitHub Discussions](https://github.com/mahdiklidari190/SEAR/discussions) tab.

## 📧 Direct Contact
For sensitive issues (e.g., security vulnerabilities), please refer to our [Security Policy](SECURITY.md) or contact the maintainer directly via [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/in/bardia-klidari-64a32a30b?originalSubdomain=ir).
