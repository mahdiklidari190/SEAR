Purpose

SEARBot is a professional SEO analysis and website crawling engine developed by Dark Wolf Agency. It is designed to:

  • Analyze on-page SEO elements (Titles, Meta descriptions, Headings).
  • Identify technical SEO issues (Broken links, redirect chains, canonical tags).
  • Estimate Core Web Vitals risks based on HTML structure.

Crawl Behavior & Compliance

SEARBot is built to be a good citizen of the web:

  • It strictly respects robots.txt directives (both Allow and Disallow).
  • It enforces a default crawl-delay of 1 second between requests to avoid overloading your server.
  • It honors the Crawl-delay directive if specified in your robots.txt.

Blocking the Bot

If you wish to block SEARBot from accessing your website, simply add the following lines to your robots.txt file:

User-agent: SEARBot
Disallow: /
